Key Comparative Inquiries

Important historiographical discussions frequently analyzed in global comparative history.

Mercantile Networks

Trans-Saharan vs. Silk Road

While the Eurasian Silk Road linked China to the Mediterranean via overland camel caravans, West African trans-Saharan routes linked the goldfields of Bambuk and Bure to North Africa, Cairo, and Venice, carrying salt, books, textiles, and precious metals.

Scholarly Centers

Timbuktu, Bologna & Nalanda

In the 12th–15th centuries, scholarship was polycentric. While medieval universities like Bologna and Paris crystallized scholasticism, the University of Sankore in Timbuktu housed 25,000 scholars, and Nalanda in India preserved Buddhist logic and mathematics.

Labor Systems

Chattel Slavery vs. Traditional Bondage

Unlike ancient Roman servitude, Asian bonded labor, or pre-Atlantic African tributary servitude, the transatlantic chattel slave system legally reduced human beings into hereditary movable property, stripped of legal personhood and codified strictly by racial hierarchy.
