FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding educational usage, citations, and travel planning.

Can educators freely use this site's timelines and comparative matrices?

Yes! All materials on Africa & The World are published under an Open Access educational framework. Teachers, lecturers, and educational non-profits are free to use our charts, timelines, and reading guides in class sessions, syllabus development, and assignments. Please include proper credit and citation.

How can researchers suggest additional books or primary source records?

We actively welcome recommendations from historians, Africanists, and educators. Please submit suggestions using the contact form above under "Archival Manuscript or Research Contribution". Please include author, publication date, historical context, and any open-access digital repositories where the source can be accessed.

Does the AI Travel Assistant provide real-time consular or visa verification?

No. While our Gemini-powered AI Travel Assistant is knowledgeable about historical landmarks, cultural etiquette, and itinerary suggestions, it does not have live visa or immigration authority feeds. Travelers must confirm all visa rules, health requirements (such as Yellow Fever certificates), and security advisories directly with official government foreign offices and embassies.

How do donations and sponsorships work?

Contributions directly cover server hosting costs, AI API infrastructure fees, and freelance historical researcher honorariums. To discuss corporate sponsorship, educational grants, or philanthropic donations, please fill out our contact form or follow the patron links above.
