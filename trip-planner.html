FEATURED ITINERARIES

Iconic African Cultural Trails

Curated thematic journeys connecting landmark UNESCO World Heritage sites across the continent.

West Africa

Atlantic Memorial Trail

Trace the memory of the diaspora: Gorée Island and the Maison des Esclaves (Senegal), Cape Coast & Elmina Castles (Ghana), the Gate of No Return at Ouidah (Benin), and the National Museum of Lagos (Nigeria).

East Africa

Swahili Maritime Route

Experience centuries of Indian Ocean trade: Lamu Old Town (Kenya), Fort Jesus in Mombasa, Stone Town of Zanzibar, and the medieval coral palaces of Kilwa Kisiwani (Tanzania).

Nile & Highlands

Nile & Rock-Hewn Sanctuary

From the Pyramids of Giza and Valley of the Kings (Egypt) to the royal pyramids of Meroë (Sudan) and the 12th-century monolithic rock churches of Lalibela (Ethiopia).
